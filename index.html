FAIRINO's window:La ventana de FAIRINO:FAIRINO 的机会窗口： The premium segment (UR+ABB+FANUC) controls ~60% of the market at USD 35–65k/unit. The value gap below USD 32k is where DOBOT operates — and where FAIRINO enters with higher payloads (up to FR30 at 30kg) and a dedicated welding line that no value-tier competitor offers locally. El segmento premium (UR+ABB+FANUC) controla ~60% del mercado a USD 35–65k/unidad.
